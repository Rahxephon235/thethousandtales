The Thousand Tales

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

What this covers

This policy covers "The Thousand Tales", a private publishing tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel The Thousand Tales. The tool is used by the channel owner only. It is not offered to the public.

What the tool does

The tool uses YouTube API Services. When the channel owner signs in with the Google account that owns the channel, the tool can:

  • upload videos to the owner's own channel;
  • set and edit the titles, descriptions, thumbnails and visibility of those videos;
  • read the owner's own channel and video analytics.

What data is accessed and stored

The tool accesses only the YouTube data of the channel owner's own account, and only for the purposes listed above. The sign-in tokens issued by Google are stored on the channel owner's own computer. They are not sent to any other server.

The tool does not collect, store or process personal data from viewers, subscribers or any other person.

Sharing

No data accessed through YouTube API Services is sold, shared or transferred to any third party.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Removing access makes the stored tokens useless. The tokens can also be deleted from the computer where they are kept.

YouTube and Google terms

By using this tool the channel owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
