The Thousand Tales

A thousand tales. One voice. Always another story.

The Thousand Tales is a YouTube channel that retells long web novels as narrated audiobook episodes. The current series is a retelling of A Record of a Mortal's Journey to Immortality by Wang Yu.

Watch on YouTube

About this app

"The Thousand Tales" is also the name of a small private tool that the channel owner uses to publish to this channel. It connects to YouTube through YouTube API Services to upload the channel's own videos, set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read the channel's own analytics.

The tool is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public and nobody else is asked to sign in to it.
